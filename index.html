FAQ

Questions you might have

Is BudgetFlow really free?
Yes — completely free, forever. No premium plan, no credit card required, no hidden charges.
How do the WhatsApp reminders work?
Add your WhatsApp number once in Settings. From the next payday, reminders go out automatically — no action needed.
Is my financial data safe?
Yes. Your data is fully encrypted and private — only you can see your own records. We take your financial privacy seriously.
Do I need to install anything?
No. BudgetFlow is a web app — it works on any browser on your phone or desktop. Just bookmark the URL.
Can I change my budget plan later?
Yes — go to Settings any time to update your income, payday date, or any budget line.
